# Review: w7-content drafts (essay, HN plan, marketing foundation), 2026-10-25

Reviewer: Fable agent, hostile but fair, tone and accuracy only. Read-only apart from this file. Standard: `docs/brand-voice.md` (24 Oct). Sources checked: `reports/grades.md` (25 Oct, v0.31.0), `docs/scoring.md`, decisions 0009/0030/0033, `docs/design/2026-10-02-quality-harness-catalogue.md`, `docs/design/2026-10-02-learning-from-aligned-pairs.md`, `docs/design/lessons.md`, `docs/CHANGELOG.md`, `factory/reviews/review-mueller-notes-2026-10-25.md`, site PRs #309 and #389 (`gh pr view`), bead `reportsthatmatter-fkx` and `-vs2x`, the 20 report repos' `golden.yaml` and `reference/`, `marketing/queue.yaml`, PR #359, the live Bluesky profile, `src/templates/layout.ts` and `processing.ts`, and the marketing repo's `strategy/` and `research/`.

**Overall.** The essay is close: dry, numbered, first person, and most of its figures check out to the digit. Its two real faults are one factual error about word accuracy that an HN reader would catch from `docs/scoring.md`, and timidity of placement: the strongest fact (half the reports fail our own bar) sits at line 49 behind a throat-clearing second paragraph. The HN plan is a good fact sheet with one self-contradiction: it tells Rufus to paste a fixed three-sentence AI line into an HN comment, which is the thing HN bans. The foundation is honest about what is open, but its first proof point breaks two of the brand voice's own bans, its primary CTA leads to a list nobody can yet send to, and one credit claim is wrong.

Findings, most important first. Line numbers are the file's. `E` = essay (`rtm-textright-1024/content/posts/getting-the-text-right.md`), `H` = HN plan (`marketing-hnplan-1024/drafts/2026-11-10/hn-essay.md`), `F` = foundation (`marketing-foundation-1024/foundation.md`), `B` = 90-day bets.

## Must fix before 10 Nov

1. **E L53, H L47: "for 18 of the 20 reports we have no clean edition, so whether every word is right is not measured at all" is wrong, and the essay contradicts it itself at L39.** Ten report repos carry a `reference/` clean edition (9/11, Saville, Philip Morris, Hillsborough, Chilcot, Columbia, Covid, Grenfell, Post Office, plus Duelfer unpublished). What is true is narrower: the **words** are scored for only 2 of 20 (9/11 and Chilcot, against proofread Wikisource pages; `docs/scoring.md` W row, decision 0035), and shown, not graded. Rewrite E L53: "And the words themselves are scored for only two of the 20 reports, the 9/11 Commission and Chilcot's executive summary, where proofread Wikisource pages exist to score against. For the other 18, word accuracy is sampled, not measured." Rewrite H L47 to match ("word accuracy is measured on 2 of 20").
2. **H L48: the fact sheet tells Rufus to post the AI line "in these words" in the first comment.** That is three sentences of agent-fixed text pasted into an HN comment, which is exactly what H L9 quotes dang as banning ("not even a tiny bit"). The brand-voice wording is for our own channels. Rewrite L48 as facts, not words: "AI, once, in your own words. The facts: no language model wrote, summarised or rewrote any report text; AI coding agents wrote much of the code and the site under your direction, and the code is public; the introductions were drafted with AI help, checked by you, and are labelled on the page. Say it before someone finds `AGENTS.md`." Same at H L71 ("The AI line above" → "the facts above").
3. **E L17 and L49: the strongest fact is buried.** L17 is a throat-clearing triplet that restates the title ("what goes wrong..., how we now measure it, and what is still wrong"), and the fact that carries the piece, ten of 20 reports fail our own bar, arrives at L49 after 30 lines of method. Replace L17 with: "Ten of the 20 reports on this site fail the bar we set for them. This is how we know, and what is still wrong." Then L49 reads as the payoff rather than a confession. The frontmatter summary (L5) has the same triplet; cut it to: "The words in an official PDF are mostly right. The structure is not. How we grade every report on the errors a reader meets, and why ten of 20 are below our own bar."
4. **F L52: the first proof point breaks two bans in the voice section 19 lines below it.** "Every paragraph of **every report** has its own link and **printed page**": brand voice L38 bans "every report" as a claim and "printed page" where the pages are the PDF's (several reports are cited by PDF page). Rewrite: "Each paragraph has its own link and its page number; the official PDF is one click away; where they differ the PDF is right."
5. **F L48, L109, B row 1: the single CTA is real on the page but not behind it.** "Subscribe to the monthly newsletter" writes a row to D1 and nothing else happens (F L103: no provider, no welcome, no confirmation; decision 0033 provider open). And add to F §8 one line on what a subscriber gets today: nothing until the provider exists.
6. **F L10: "Datopian (credited for hosting and engineering on `/about`)" is not true.** The only Datopian credit in `src/` or `content/` is the site footer in `src/templates/layout.ts` L144 ("built with ❤️ by Rufus Pollock and Datopian, who have worked on open data since 2015"); `/about` does not name Datopian.
