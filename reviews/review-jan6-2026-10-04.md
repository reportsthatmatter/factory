## Editorial: neutrality and verbatim accuracy

I read every finding, excerpt, reading-guide item, highlight and card against `pnpm paragraphs us-jan6-committee`. I also checked every background fact that is not in the report against outside sources. Our wording is neutral and attributed: "the committee concludes", "it describes", "the report finds". It uses no adjectives of judgment. It says what the report is and is not ("a committee's recommendations, not charges; ... not a court's"). It also records the other side: the composition dispute, which the report itself prints at p. 128-130, and the 2024 House Administration subcommittee reports that criticise the committee.

Fixed in `4fa50bfc`:

1. **The background said "The committee held its hearings in 2022."** This is wrong: its first hearing, with four police officers, was on 27 July 2021. It now reads: "Its first hearing, in July 2021, heard police officers who had defended the Capitol; its main public hearings ran from June to October 2022."
2. **The Pence finding said the pressure was something "the President's own lawyers told him Pence could not lawfully do".** That paraphrases a judgment, and the report's words are different (p. 33). It now quotes them: "no White House lawyer believed Pence could lawfully refuse to count electoral votes." `jacob-recorded-eastman-s-admission`, the excerpt's paragraph, is now among its cites.
3. **The 187-minutes finding's judgment is now a quotation:** it calls those 187 minutes "a dereliction of duty" (p. 577).
4. **The reading guide said the recommendations run "from the Electoral Count Act to threats against election workers".** That is recommendation 8 of 11. It now ends "to the Insurrection Act" (11 of 11, counted from `full.md`).
5. **The Giuliani card read "he admitted during his Select Committee deposition that 'I do not think the machines stole the election.'"** It is verbatim, but on a card or a Bluesky post "he" names nobody. The sentence that names him is too long for the 300-grapheme limit with the source line.

## Getting better faster (reviewer retro)

- **What slowed me down:**
  - `pnpm posts` exits 1 on main for other reports' over-long quotes. That noise hides a real new failure: my 309-grapheme card was one line among 20.
  - `verify.sh` without `VERIFY_SHARED=1` fails `aggregate` in a one-report worktree setup, which cost a restart.
  - **Peer-safety slip:** I stopped my first verify with `pkill -f scripts/verify.sh`. That pattern would also have killed any other agent's `verify.sh` running at the time. Use TaskStop, or the PID, next time.
- **What would have caught these earlier:**
  - `pnpm editorial` could warn when a `card: true` quote opens with a lower-case pronoun.
  - It could also warn when the yaml's background has no source comment for a sentence with a date that is not in the report.
  - The landing renderer had no test with a quotation inside a quotation in curly-quote style.
- **One proposal:** `pnpm posts --check` should fail only on this change's items (a `--report <id>` filter), so a new report's queue problems are not drowned out by the corpus's standing skips.
