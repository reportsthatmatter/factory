# Review: docs/brand-voice.md (24 Oct 2026)

**Verdict:** the stance is right and the Don'ts are good, but two of the five model lines break the strategy or the project's own facts, the stance stops one sentence short of distinctive, and five everyday situations are unaddressed. A morning's work.

1. **"Silent about verdicts" is not distinctive; every archive says it.** What is distinctive is that our opinion is what we choose to quote, and the doc never says how. Two agents handed Challenger will pick different sentences. Add: "We don't comment on findings; we choose which to quote, and that is the opinion. Quote what the report itself calls a finding, conclusion or recommendation. Never the sentence that sounds worst."

2. **The access opinion is one argument and runs out by Wednesday.** Rule: "An opinion about access always comes with one number from our own work" (Leveson: 983,000 words, 74 headings).

3. **Line 1 over-claims the product.** "Link to the sentence": the site links to the paragraph. Rewrite: "Public inquiries are the most careful fact-finding a country does, and then they come out as one PDF nobody can link into. I've been rebuilding them so every paragraph has its own address."

4. **Line 2 breaks the doc's own Do #6.** Challenger is `affected_community: true` (strategy §8); every post from it carries a context line, and the model line has none. Add one ("House Committee on Science and Technology (1986). Seven crew died.") or use a non-affected report. p. 146 checks out; show the report's trailing ellipsis.

5. **Line 4 is false.** `content/posts/getting-the-text-right.md` says Leveson had 74 headings, now 87, and "recovering the rest needs a piece of work of its own". Rewrite: "The Leveson PDF reports its chapter titles as 'ChapTer 1' and 'parT a'. 983,000 words, 74 headings. We've got it to 87. The rest is a job of its own." Keep a line like this.

6. **Line 5 contradicts strategy §8**: the January 6th report "is quoted only on its own news days and never first". The midterms are not its news day, and the doc's own rule bans "a claim about an election" then uses an election as the hook. p. 691 is right; the hook is not. Delete, and tighten the Don't: "The date can be the hook only when it is the report's own: publication anniversary, a court day about the report itself." Replace with US v. Philip Morris on a tobacco ruling day.

7. **"The decision 0009 wording" does not exist**; 0009 says only that an /about note is in progress. Put the sentence here, from §8: "No language model touched the report text. AI agents built the software and the site. The introductions and highlights are ours: AI-assisted, editor-approved, labelled." Once, then stop.

8. **Missing: the one-line description.** Decision 0017 changed /about to "The report's own words, as published. Our introductions and highlights are labelled as ours." State it, and ban "No commentary. No spin." and "Just the source", which still sit in the January plans.

9. **Missing: titles and subject lines.** Strategy: "title names reports, not features". Add: "A title is the report, the year, then one thing: a page or what we did. 'Columbia Accident Investigation Board (2003), p. 195'. No reveals, exposes, shows; no question titles."

10. **Missing: criticism and takedowns.** Line 3 covers our errors; nothing covers hostile replies or removal requests. Add: "Reply once, under 40 words, with the fix, the log entry or the removal done. Never argue a finding. Takedown from an affected person: do it, say 'Done', log it, ask no reason."

11. **Missing: house style.** British spelling in our text, the report's own in quotes. "p. 146" for a printed page; "para 2.11.56" where the report numbers paragraphs, and the paragraph wins; "PDF p." when the number is the file's. Figures for numbers. Year in brackets on first mention only.
